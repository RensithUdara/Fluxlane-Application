# 🌱 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Fluxlane to be a welcoming and respectful project for everyone.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to participate with kindness, patience, and good faith.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Give clear feedback without personal attacks.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Help newcomers when possible.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that makes participation unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code.
